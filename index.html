About Sharon Makeup Lounge

Your Premium Destination for Beauty & Transformation

With over 23 years of experience, Sharon Makeup Lounge has been a trusted distributor and supplier of premium beauty, makeup, haircare, skincare, salon essentials, and professional equipment. From our beginnings as Saundarya – The Beauty Shop to becoming Sharon Makeup Lounge, our commitment to quality, authenticity, and customer satisfaction has remained unchanged.

  • 100% Authentic Curation: All cosmetic ranges are direct imports from certified international brands.
  • Expert Consultation: Our lounge provides personalized tone-matching and technique styling.
  • Cruelty-Free Focus: We prioritize formulas that are ethical, vegan-friendly, and skin-enriching.
